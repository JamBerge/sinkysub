Sinky Sub — Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

The short version

Sinky Sub has no accounts, no analytics, and does not track you. It does show ads, and the ad network receives a device identifier in order to serve them. Nothing you do in the game is tied to your identity, and nothing follows you into other apps.

What is stored, and where

The game saves your progress:

  • your best score and last score
  • how many medals you have earned
  • which submarine hulls you have unlocked, and which one is equipped
  • your music, sound, haptics and random-hull settings

All of it lives in your device's own storage. Most of it — your best score, your medals, your unlocked hulls and your settings — also syncs to your own iCloud account, so a new phone picks up where the old one left off. That copy belongs to you, sits under your Apple Account, and is covered by Apple's privacy policy; the developer cannot see it or read it. Deleting the app removes the copy on the device. The iCloud copy is removed through your device's iCloud storage settings.

Advertising

The game shows a banner supplied by Google AdMob. Two things about how it is set up are worth stating plainly.

The ads are non-personalised. Every request the game makes asks Google for contextual ads only. No profile of you is built or used, which is why the app never asks for permission to track you.

Google receives a device identifier and basic advertising data — enough to serve a banner, count that it was shown, and avoid repeating the same one. It is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. Google's own handling of it is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Ad content is restricted to a general-audience rating, and sensitive categories are blocked.

Buying Remove Ads ends this. Once the purchase is active the game makes no ad requests at all.

Network use

Apart from those ad requests, the app talks only to Apple: iCloud, for the progress sync described above, and the App Store, for the Remove Ads purchase. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store and StoreKit. The developer never sees your payment details, your Apple Account, or any other identifying information — only whether this device is entitled to the purchase.

Children

Sinky Sub is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects ever changes, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will be updated to match.
