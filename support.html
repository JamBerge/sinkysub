Sinky Sub — Support

A one-tap arcade game for iPhone

How to play

Your submarine floats upward on its own. Tap anywhere to fire the thrusters and dive. Thread the gaps in the coral and stay off the cave roof — that is the only surface that ends a run. The sandy floor is scenery; sinking into it is safe.

Common questions

Some submarines show "???" instead of how to unlock them.
That is intended. A couple of hulls are hidden and unlock by other means.

I bought Remove Ads and got a new phone.
Open Settings inside the game and choose Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account rather than to a device, so this works on any phone signed in to the same account.

My progress disappeared.
Progress is stored on the device only, so deleting the app deletes it. There is no cloud backup.
